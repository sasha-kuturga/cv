MTS — national campaign

Assistant Producer — TV & digital commercial, CG/VFX-heavy

A VFX-driven commercial for one of Russia's largest telecom operators, carried from client brief to national broadcast in two months.

  • Bidding and vendors. Built cost estimates, scoped the CG/VFX and motion design work, and sourced and negotiated with a vendor and freelance pool that came to roughly 20 people and suppliers across the campaign.
  • A full CG rebuild, without moving the air date. Across five rounds of client feedback the graphics were called back to be rebuilt from scratch. I re-scoped the remaining work, re-sequenced the vendor schedule and rebalanced the budget — and the spot aired on the original date.
  • On set. Worked directly with the CG and VFX supervisors so the shoot delivered what post actually needed: plates, reference, on-set data and tracking coverage.
  • Post and delivery. Ran review rounds and version control through comp and finishing, and QC'd against broadcast and platform specs. Delivered a 1920×1080 broadcast master plus vertical cutdowns for social.
