2025 · Dragon-fly Studio · internal tooling

Production visibility, automated

Designed and implemented — production reporting system

Daily status reporting was taking roughly two hours and still left gaps. I designed a system that connects artist availability, allocated hours, task ownership, dependencies, submissions, approvals and client feedback into a single live view.

  • Cut daily reporting from about two hours to 45 minutes — roughly 60% of the time back, every day, for the whole production side.
  • Replaced manual status chasing with a view leads and clients can read themselves, so blockers surface the day they appear rather than at the next check-in.
  • Built on the tools already in the pipeline — Jira and Jira Automation, Confluence, ShotGrid and Perforce — rather than adding a system anyone had to learn from scratch.
